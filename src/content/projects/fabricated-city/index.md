*Fabricated City*, directed by Kwang-Hyun Park, is a South Korean crime action film. I joined the
post-production as a **VFX compositor** at **Dexter Studios**.

## Synopsis

An unemployed young man who spends his days as the leader of an online game team is framed for a
murder he did not commit. After escaping prison, he and his gaming teammates set out to uncover who
fabricated the case against him.
